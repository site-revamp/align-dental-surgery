Before your appointment

A little clarity.
A more comfortable start.

Have a question about insurance, your child’s first appointment or which treatment to explore? Ask our team before you book.

Insurance & direct billing

Direct billing arrangements are available with participating insurers. CHAS and MediSave apply to eligible treatments. Please check your benefits and eligibility with the clinic.

Can I use my dental insurance?

Align offers direct billing with participating insurers. Share your insurer and plan details with the clinic so the team can help check the arrangements before your visit.

Do you see children as well as adults?

Yes. Align provides children’s dentistry alongside care for adults. Explore children’s dental care.

Can I discuss replacing a missing tooth?

Yes. Dental implants and bridges are among the available services. Your dentist can assess your needs and explain suitable options. Learn about dental implants.

Do I need to choose a treatment before booking?

No. Call or WhatsApp the team to explain your concern and ask which appointment would be appropriate.

Where can I find treatment aftercare?

Visit the Align Aftercare Hub for procedure-specific guidance. Contact the clinic if you need advice about your own recovery.
