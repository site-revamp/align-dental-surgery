# Align Dental Surgery — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Align Dental Surgery is a dental practice at Forum The Shopping Mall on Orchard Road, Singapore. It serves families, professionals and expatriate residents, offering general and cosmetic dentistry, implants, Invisalign, children’s dentistry, specialist dentistry and airway-focused orthodontics.

The redesigned homepage brings the introduction and appointment options alongside the opening photograph, improves readability and simplifies navigation around patient needs.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. Introduction and Appointment Options Beside the Photograph

The main message and appointment options now sit beside the opening photograph. Previously, the photograph pushed this information down the page, requiring visitors to scroll. The revised layout presents the practice’s introduction and next steps together immediately.

### 2. Larger Supporting Typography

Larger, more readable typography replaces supporting labels as small as 12px and service descriptions around 15px. This makes labels, treatment information and supporting content easier to read and scan.

### 3. Simpler Navigation

Navigation is organised around care, dentists, visit planning and contact. This replaces multiple extensive dropdowns that presented too many choices at once, helping visitors locate relevant information more easily.

## Design Improvements

- **Layout and hierarchy:** The photograph, main message and appointment options form a coordinated opening section.
- **Typography:** Larger supporting text improves readability across labels and service descriptions.
- **Consistency:** Clear navigation categories create a coherent structure for exploring the practice.
- **User experience:** Appointment options appear earlier, with fewer competing navigation choices.
